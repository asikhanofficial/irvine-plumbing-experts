Why Snaking Fails Where Jetting Succeeds

Standard rooter service Irvine relies on a mechanical blade to cut through solid objects like tree roots or paper towels. However, Irvine's hard water causes a build-up of mineral scale that adheres to the pipe walls. Additionally, kitchen grease cools and solidifies into a wax-like substance that snaking simply cannot remove. Our licensed plumbers in Irvine recommend hydro jetting because it effectively acts like a "pressure wash" for your plumbing system, restoring it to a like-new state.
