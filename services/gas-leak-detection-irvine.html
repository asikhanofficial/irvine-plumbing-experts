Critical Warning Signs of a Gas Leak

Because natural gas is naturally odorless and colorless, utility companies add a chemical called mercaptan to make it smell like "rotten eggs." If you notice any of these signs, you need an emergency plumber Irvine immediately:

  • The "Rotten Egg" Odor: This is the most common indicator of a leak in your stove, furnace, or outdoor line.
  • Hissing Sounds: A whistling or hissing noise near a gas appliance or meter indicates a pressurized line has failed.
  • Dying Houseplants: Natural gas prevents a plant's roots from absorbing oxygen. If your indoor plants are dying without reason, gas may be present.

Our Advanced Gas Detection Technology

Traditional methods like soapy water are still useful, but for hidden leaks inside walls or under concrete slabs, we use professional plumbing services Irvine grade technology:

  • Electronic Gas Sniffers: Sensors that can detect parts-per-million concentrations of combustible gas.
  • Ultrasonic Sound Sensors: Microphones that "hear" the unique frequency of escaping gas.
  • Inert Gas Tracer Testing: Pressurizing the lines with nitrogen to see if they hold pressure over a 24-hour period.
  • Thermal Imaging: Checking for cold spots near gas lines which can indicate an expanding gas leak.
