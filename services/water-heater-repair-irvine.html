The Impact of Irvine’s Hard Water on Your Heater

Irvine is known for having a high mineral content in its municipal water supply. This "hard water" leads to sediment buildup at the bottom of traditional tanks. Over time, this sediment creates an insulating layer that forces your heater to work harder, leading to popping sounds, higher utility bills, and eventual tank failure. Our licensed plumbers in Irvine specialize in sediment flushing and anode rod replacement to extend the life of your unit. If your system is beyond repair, we also provide expert water heater installation Irvine services.

Signs You Need Water Heater Repair Irvine Now

Ignoring minor issues can lead to a catastrophic flood. If you notice any of these signs, contact an emergency plumber Irvine immediately:

  • No Hot Water: If your water stays cold despite the heater running, you likely have a failed heating element or a tripped circuit.
  • Popping or Rumbling Noises: These sounds are caused by boiling water trapped under heavy sediment layers at the bottom of the tank.
  • Rusty or Discolored Water: This is a sign that the interior of your tank is corroding, which can lead to a sudden rupture.

Safety first: We check pressure relief valves and gas connections on every service call.
