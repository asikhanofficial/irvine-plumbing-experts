When Is Replacement Necessary?

It can be difficult for homeowners to know when to stop repairing and start replacing. As a licensed plumber Irvine, we provide honest assessments based on sewer camera inspection data. Replacement is typically the best option if:

  • Chronic Clogs: If you are calling for drain cleaning Irvine multiple times a year for the same line, the pipe is likely failing structurally.
  • Pinhole Leaks & Corrosion: Older cast iron pipes rust from the inside out, leading to numerous leaks that simple patches cannot fix.

Modern Trenchless Sewer Replacement Irvine

The biggest fear homeowners have with replacement is the destruction of their property. We eliminate this fear using trenchless sewer replacement techniques. By utilizing "Pipe Bursting," we pull a brand-new, high-density polyethylene (HDPE) pipe through your old one, breaking the old pipe apart while simultaneously installing the new one. This method is faster, cleaner, and more durable than traditional digging.

The Benefits of Professional Replacement

Hiring the best plumber in Irvine for your replacement project offers several key advantages:

  • 50+ Year Lifespan: Modern HDPE and PVC pipes are resistant to chemical corrosion and root intrusion.
  • Increased Property Value: A documented sewer line replacement is a major selling point for Irvine real estate.
  • Improved Flow: New pipes have smooth interior walls, significantly reducing the chances of clogs.
  • Environmentally Safe: Our seamless pipes prevent raw sewage from leaching into the Southern California groundwater.
