Signs You Need Sewer Repair Irvine

Sewer problems often begin with subtle warnings before escalating into full-blown emergencies. If you notice any of the following signs, it is time to call a professional:

  • Soggy Patches in Yard: Standing water or unusually green grass over your sewer line indicates a leak.
  • Foul Odors: The smell of rotten eggs or sewer gas inside your home is a major red flag.
  • Multiple Backups: If sinks, toilets, and showers are all backing up simultaneously, the issue is likely in the main line.
  • Gurgling Toilets: Trapped air caused by a partial obstruction leads to bubbling and gurgling sounds.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Trenchless Sewer Repair?

Trenchless repair allows us to fix or replace your sewer line without digging up your entire yard. We use existing access points to insert a new lining, saving you time and landscaping costs.

Can tree roots destroy my sewer line?

Yes, in Irvine's lush neighborhoods, tree roots are a primary cause of sewer damage. They seek out moisture and can enter the pipe through tiny joints, eventually cracking the line.
