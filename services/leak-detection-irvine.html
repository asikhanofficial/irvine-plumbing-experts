Common Signs of a Hidden Water Leak

Water leaks often hide in plain sight. If you notice any of these signs, you need a licensed plumber Irvine to perform a pressure test:

  • Unexplained High Water Bills: If your usage hasn't changed but your bill has spiked, water is likely escaping somewhere in your system.
  • Warm Spots on the Floor: A warm area on your kitchen or hallway floor usually indicates a hot-water line slab leak repair Irvine is needed.
  • The Sound of Running Water: If you hear a faint hissing or rushing sound when all faucets are turned off, a pipe is actively leaking.

Our Non-Invasive Detection Methods

Being the best plumber in Irvine means investing in the best tools. Our diagnostic arsenal includes:
