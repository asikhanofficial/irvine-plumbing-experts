Why Pipes Leak in Orange County

Southern California’s municipal water is often high in mineral content, commonly referred to as "hard water." Over decades, these minerals react with copper pipes, causing a chemical reaction known as pitting corrosion. This results in tiny pinhole leaks that are difficult to see but can cause massive structural damage and mold. We specialize in pipe repair Irvine and complete repiping Irvine services using high-quality PEX materials that are immune to this type of corrosion.
