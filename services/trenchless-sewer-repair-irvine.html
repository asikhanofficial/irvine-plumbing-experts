How Trenchless Technology Works

There are two primary forms of trenchless sewer repair Irvine: Pipe Lining and Pipe Bursting. Our local plumber Irvine team will perform a sewer camera inspection to determine which method is right for your home’s specific failure:

  • CIPP Pipe Lining: We insert a flexible, epoxy-soaked liner into your existing pipe. Once in place, it is inflated and "cured," creating a hard, seamless shell that seals clogs and cracks forever.
  • Pipe Bursting: For collapsed pipes, we pull a new, high-density polyethylene (HDPE) pipe through the old one. The old pipe is shattered (burst) as the new, stronger pipe is pulled into its place.

We monitor the curing process with thermal sensors to ensure a 100% watertight seal.
